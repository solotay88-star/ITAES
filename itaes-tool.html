IT Asset EOS Scanner (ITAES)

Helps an IT Auditor detect assets that may have reached End of Life or End of Support. One file, runs in this browser only. Each scan is standalone.

Privacy: Your asset file is never uploaded — it is parsed only in this browser. Only product name, brand and version are sent to public sources to look up End of Support.
For the office: live End of Support checks need internet access to endoflife.date and eosl.ai, plus the vendor domains listed in the mapping file. If those are blocked, parsing, the table and export still work; each asset will show the fetch failure and fall back to the mapping file or a manual check.
